# 047 The screen bezel and the controller
Status: accepted (2026-11-16, T44)
Context: T44 puts the game canvas in a bezel and turns the touch overlay into the device's own controller (docs/PHASE2.md). The overlay covered the bottom of the picture, drew placeholder buttons, and answered only pointer events, so VoiceOver and Switch Control activation did nothing.
Decision: #game-view is a grid: in landscape the D-pad column, the screen and the face column side by side, with Select and Start under the D-pad and the face keys; in portrait the screen on top and D-pad, Select, Start and the face keys in one row under it. #pad stays one element (the Controller group, the pointer listener, isPageKey's #pad test) and lays its keys into the game view's tracks with subgrid; it ignores pointers and its keys take them. The bezel is a .plate in --well, sunk instead of raised, holding the canvas, #cart-error as a coral plate over the screen's top, and a strip with a second .power-led and the FES badge. fitCanvas fits the canvas into #screen-slot minus the bezel's chrome, measured as the bezel's size minus the canvas's, so only CSS says how thick the bezel is. #game-view's width is rounded down to an even number of px (100vw, because WebKit's round() mishandles one percentage argument) and the bezel sits at the top of its slot, so centring never lands on half a pixel (decision 045). Hit areas keep their sizes (56 px D-pad cells with diagonal corners, 72 px A and B); Select and Start grow from 88×32 to 88×44 px. The D-pad is five cells painted as one cross, each arm outlined on its outer sides and the centre inked at its four corners so the outline turns the inner corners; a held arm darkens. A, B, Select and Start are a mask of centred bars (a stepped circle, a stepped pill) on five stacked boxes, skirt, ink, highlight, shade and face, each 1u smaller, which gives an exact 1u outline and rule 3's bands with no art files or filters. The pills are straight: rotating blurs edges, and stepped bars shifted sideways break the 1u outline at each step. data-held comes from the mask sent to Swift each frame, so touch, keyboard and assistive presses all show. A click on a pad key while a pad pointer is down, or within 500 ms of one ending, belongs to that finger and is ignored; any other click (VoiceOver, Switch Control, Space on a focused key) holds the key for 6 console frames, so btnp is true on the first. Pad keys keep tabindex="-1": every console button is on the keyboard already, and assistive technology reaches them through the accessibility tree. Game scale (no install hint) drops from 5× to 4× at 1194×834, from 6× to 5× at 1366×1024 and 1376×1032, from 4× to 3× at 790×834 and from 3× to 2× at 600×834; 1133×744 and 834×1194 stay 4×, 744×1133 3×. The owner accepted all four drops on #113.
Alternatives: the overlay over the picture (the brief makes the controller hardware beside it); display: contents on #pad (WebKit has dropped such elements from the accessibility tree); a second grid template for the pad (two templates to keep in step); clip-path shapes (the border is cut at each step, so the outline breaks); filter: drop-shadow outlines (reported soft in iOS Safari, and it outlines the labels too); event.detail or pointerType to recognise assistive clicks (WebKit reports touch clicks as "mouse"; VoiceOver's detail is undocumented); data-held from pointer events only (misses click and keyboard presses, and needs a second update path).
